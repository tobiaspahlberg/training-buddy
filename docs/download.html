Installing the Android app

  1. Tap Download the app above. The file lands in your Downloads folder, named TrainingBuddy.apk.
  2. Open the downloaded file – from the notification, or from Downloads in your file manager.
  3. Allow this source to install apps. Android asks once, for the app you are opening the file from – your browser or your file manager. Tap Settings, turn the switch on, and come back.
  4. Install anyway, if Play Protect says the app is unknown. It says that about every app that did not come from the Play Store; the warning is about where it came from, not what it does.
  5. Open Training Buddy, pick a category, and start a session.

Later versions install over the top. Downloading a newer APK and opening it keeps everything you have made – your own sessions, plans and history. There is no need to uninstall first, and the app itself tells you when there is a new one.

Using the web version

  1. Tap Open the web version above. That is the whole of it – it runs in the browser and remembers what you make on that device.
  2. Add it to your home screen to have it open full screen, without the browser round it. On Android: the browser menu → Add to home screen. On iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Start once before you put the phone down. Browsers stay silent until you have touched the page, so the first tap is what lets it speak at all.

Keep the screen on, or use headphones. A phone is free to mute a browser tab once the screen turns off, and some do. If you want the voice with the phone in your pocket, the Android app is the one to have – that is the reason it exists.

What it is for

Built-in plans to work through week by week, single workouts to run as they stand, and an editor for making your own – steps, intervals and circuits, in whatever order you like. Everything you make stays on your own device, and can be saved to a file and read back on another one. No account, no adverts, nothing sent anywhere.
