About

Training Buddy

An interval timer for training and rehab programs. Times are announced out loud so you can keep the phone in your pocket.

Voice

Use headphones for the most reliable audio. On the web version the phone may mute speech once the screen turns off — the Android app uses the phone's own speech engine.

Before the first step

Time to put the phone down and get to the start after you tap Start. The clock waits, counts you in out loud, and only then begins the first step.

Your data

Everything you have made lives on this phone and nowhere else. A backup is one file you can put somewhere safe and read back into a new phone.

Restore

Restoring puts back what is missing and replaces what carries the same name inside the app. Nothing already on the phone is lost: a day ticked off in either copy stays ticked off, and finished sessions from both are kept.
